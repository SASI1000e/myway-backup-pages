Privacy Policy — MYWAY Backup

Last updated: November 7, 2026

MYWAY Backup is a private utility intended for the owner of MYWAY Rental Manager. Its purpose is to back up business system data to a dedicated folder in the owner's Google Drive account and support owner-approved recovery.

Information processed

Once configured and authorized, the utility may process backup archives containing rental bookings, vehicle records, customer information, operational records and database schema. It also processes Google OAuth credentials needed to upload and verify backups.

Google Drive access

The utility is designed to use a limited Google Drive permission and operate only on a dedicated backup folder and files it is authorized to access. It is not intended to read unrelated personal Drive files. Google's drive.file permission is not itself a strict folder-bound permission; application-level checks are also required.

Storage and sharing

Backups are intended to be stored in the owner's personal Google Drive, not shared with employees. No backup data is intended for public publication. Google processes files according to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Retention and automated deletion have not yet been configured. The owner controls the backup folder and can delete files or revoke the app's Google account access. Backups may remain until manually deleted or a retention policy is activated.

Security and recovery

OAuth credentials should be stored as protected secrets, not in public source code. Restore operations must require owner approval. The automation is not yet active, and this policy does not claim that backups are currently running.

Contact

For questions about this owner-operated utility, contact the MYWAY Rental Manager administrator through the existing business support channel.
